About me

I like finding connections between different interests. One day I might be working on a chess initiative, the next experimenting with code, researching artificial intelligence, or attending an international affairs event.

I started playing chess and learning computer science during the pandemic. Chess taught me to think several moves ahead, and computer science taught me to break problems down. Curiosity about other places and perspectives taught me to ask who is affected by the decisions behind the things I see.

Lately that has pulled me toward one big question: how technology, business, and public policy shape one another, especially as AI develops. I'm curious not only about what we can build, but how it gets adopted, governed, and shared around the world.

“Sometimes understanding the world starts with a simple hello.”
